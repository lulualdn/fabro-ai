Why cities are planting tiny forests

Dense native woods the size of a tennis court are popping up between buildings. Here is what they change.

CBClaire Bonnet12 March 2026

A forest in 3 years instead of 100

The Miyawaki method plants 3 to 5 native saplings per square metre. Competing for light, they grow up to ten times faster than in a classic plantation.

“After two summers, the street was 4 °C cooler in the afternoon.”

What the data says

Good to know. A tiny forest needs about 3 years of watering and weeding, then it looks after itself.

How to start one

  1. Find 100 m² of public or school land.
  2. Pick 30+ native species.
  3. Plant with volunteers in one day.
