## Fabro won’t start
Start it once more. If it crashes again at start, it starts without mods the next time. If that’s not enough, install over it: your settings and conversations are kept.

## Answers are slow
- Open Settings → Models → **Measure again**: Fabro picks the fastest engine on your machine.
- Close games or apps that use the graphics card.
- Use a smaller model day to day, and the “Ask the big model” button when needed.

## An update failed
Nothing is lost: your previous version is intact. Click **Retry**. If it fails again, download the installer from the [home page](index.html#download) and run it.

## Fabro Anywhere can’t find my PC
- Fabro must be open on the PC, with Fabro Anywhere turned on.
- Some networks (work, school) block the relays. Try on mobile data.
- As a last resort, forget the PC in Fabro Anywhere and link it again.

## My API key is refused
Check the model name: each provider has its own. Once the key is typed, the provider’s model list appears in the “Model” field.

## The microphone doesn’t work
Allow the microphone for this site in your browser settings, then reload the page.

## Uninstall
Windows: Settings → Apps → Fabro. macOS: drag Fabro to the Trash. Your data stays in the Fabro folder of your profile; delete it if you want everything gone.
