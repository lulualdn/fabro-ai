# Getting started

## Install
Download the installer for your system from the [home page](index.html#download). It measures your computer and suggests a model that suits it. You can change it later.

## Ask
There is one field. Write what you want, as long as you want, attach files or images. For a multi-step task, Fabro writes a plan and shows it above its answer.

- **Enter** sends, **Shift + Enter** adds a line.
- **/** opens commands: long task, deep research, Fabro Code.
- While Fabro works, you can add an instruction: it takes it on the way.

## Permissions
Reading and searching ask nothing. Writing a file, running a command or acting on the screen asks first, unless you pick “Always”. Publishing, deleting and paying always ask.

## Where your data is
Everything is on your computer: conversations, memory, models. Nothing goes elsewhere unless you use an online provider or Fabro Anywhere.
